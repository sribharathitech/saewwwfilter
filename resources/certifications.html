Certifications & Quality Documents

A transparent guide to requesting current, scope-specific company and product documentation for filtration-media qualification.

Quality assurance

Quality Built Into Every Product

Our manufacturing unit is ISO 9001:2000 and Dun & Bradstreet certified, reflecting our commitment to structured manufacturing, stringent process controls and consistent product quality.

From raw-material selection through manufacturing and final inspection, we follow defined quality practices to ensure our filtration products meet the required specifications and performance expectations.

For specific customer or application requirements, we also collaborate with BTRA – The Bombay Textile Research Association for independent testing, validation and certification of selected products, providing additional technical assurance wherever required.

Due diligence

What to verify on a certificate

01

Legal entity

The named organisation should match the contracting or manufacturing entity.

02

Site and scope

Check that the relevant plant and filtration activities are within scope.

03

Standard edition

Confirm the current standard and any applicable transition status.

04

Issuer

Verify accreditation and certificate authenticity with the issuing body.

05

Validity

Review issue, expiry and surveillance status.

06

Product documents

Obtain separate grade-specific contact, material or validation evidence.
