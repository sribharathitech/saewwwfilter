## Post-launch Search Console actions

1. Verify the domain property and submit `/sitemap.xml`.
2. Inspect the homepage, filter-pad hub, gelatin page and key product pages; request indexing after deployment.
3. Confirm the old URL paths resolve to their meta-refresh pages and monitor &ldquo;Page with redirect&rdquo; reports.
4. Watch indexing, canonical selection, Core Web Vitals and mobile usability weekly during the first eight weeks.
5. Review query/page reports monthly; expand pages that receive impressions but have low average position or CTR.
6. Replace legacy meta-refresh pages with true 301 redirects if hosting later moves to a platform that supports them.

## Claims requiring future evidence

- The legacy site references ISO 9001:2000 and Dun & Bradstreet certification, but current certificates were not public. The site therefore does not claim they are current.
- Alpha-cellulose values of 90% and 98% were requested in the brief but not verified on the live Sri Bharathi source. They are not stated as product specifications.
- All food, pharmaceutical, FDA, NSF or similar suitability statements must be supported by current grade-specific documents before being strengthened in copy or structured data.
