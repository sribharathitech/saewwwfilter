Operating principle

How the filtration product works

A dust-collection cartridge draws contaminated air through pleated media. Particles are retained mainly at the outer surface while clean air passes to the cartridge core. Pulse-jet or other cleaning reverses flow briefly to release the dust cake into the collector hopper.

Pleating fits a large filtration area into a compact envelope. Open pleat spacing and surface treatment influence cake release, pressure drop and cleaning-air demand.

Media design

Construction and process behaviour

Sri Bharathi publishes 100% polyester spun-bonded media formed into a cylindrical pleated pack, supported by a metal core and encapsulated with metal end caps. Sponge-rubber sealing can provide positive fitment. The media is described as thermally bonded without a binding agent.

Available treatments referenced by the source include PTFE coating or lamination, aluminium or stainless-steel antistatic coatings and fire-retardant coating. The coating must be matched to the dust hazard and operating environment.

Process fit

Applications and selection considerations

Published industries include pharmaceuticals, steel, power, powder coating, cement, chemicals, metals and minerals, and paints and pigments. Dust testing and hazard assessment are essential before selection.

Combustible-dust caution

Antistatic or fire-retardant media alone does not make a dust collector explosion safe. The site must assess dust explosibility, ignition sources, grounding, venting or suppression, isolation and applicable regulations with qualified specialists.

Questions engineers ask

Frequently asked questions

What does PTFE treatment change?

A coating or membrane can improve surface release, moisture/oil resistance or fine-particle control depending on construction. Confirm the quoted treatment.

Does more media area always improve performance?

Not if pleats are packed so tightly that dust bridges or cleaning air cannot reach the valleys. Area, pleat spacing and cleaning must be balanced.

Can a cartridge solve a combustible-dust hazard?

No. It is one component of a collector system. A formal dust-hazard and explosion-protection design is required.

What dimensions are needed for a replacement?

Provide overall length, OD, ID, end-cap design, mount type, gasket dimensions, current media and collector model.
