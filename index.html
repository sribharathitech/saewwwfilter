Filter pad expertise

Depth filtration is more than a nominal retention number

Industrial filter pads use a porous fibre matrix to capture particles throughout the thickness of the media. This depth-loading behaviour can provide useful contaminant-holding capacity and stable clarification where a thin surface screen would blind rapidly.

For a reliable recommendation, engineers must consider fibre composition, thickness, basis weight (GSM), porosity, wet and dry strength, flow behaviour, particle-retention objective and compatibility with the process fluid. Sri Bharathi supplies pulp pads, sparkler-format pads, activated carbon pads and high-temperature media, with customised dimensions available subject to application review.
