Operating principle

How the filtration product works

Industrial filter paper is a porous fibre sheet that supports fast or coarse filtration by allowing liquid to pass while retaining suspended matter at the surface and within the fibre network. A cake may form and become part of the filtration mechanism as solids accumulate.

Flow and retention must be considered together: an open sheet may deliver rapid throughput but retain fewer fine particles, while a denser sheet can improve clarification at the cost of higher resistance and shorter cycles under heavy solids load.

Selection starts with the process.

State the liquid or gas, operating temperature, target retention, batch volume, viscosity, solids load, housing geometry and cleaning or disposal requirement.

Media design

Construction and process behaviour

Sri Bharathi states that its filter papers are manufactured from virgin wood pulps, including softwood or hardwood furnish. Premium grades may receive impregnation to balance wet strength and other properties for the intended filtration duty.

Published critical parameters include porosity, bursting factor, particle-retention character and flow rate. These properties should be tied to a test method and grade tolerance before purchasing decisions are made.

Process fit

Applications and selection considerations

Industrial filter papers may be used in chemicals, pharmaceuticals, foods, beverages, cosmetics and general process filtration where a supported disposable sheet is appropriate. They can serve as gross particulate media, cake-support layers, prefilters or laboratory/process-development tools.

How to compare grades

  • Test equal effective area under the same feed, temperature and pressure.
  • Measure initial flow and total throughput to the defined endpoint.
  • Compare filtrate clarity or particle result at several points in the cycle.
  • Inspect for tearing, edge bypass, fibre release and difficult cake removal.
  • Review contact, traceability and extractables documents before regulated use.

Questions engineers ask

Frequently asked questions

Is filter paper specified only by GSM?

No. GSM indicates mass per area. Porosity, formation, thickness, fibre furnish, strength and treatment determine how the sheet behaves.

What is bursting factor?

It is a measure related to resistance to rupture under a specified test. The value is meaningful only with its method, units and whether the sample is wet or dry.

Why can laboratory flow differ from plant flow?

Viscosity, temperature, effective area, pressure, solids concentration and cake development may all differ between the test and production equipment.

Can filter paper be reused?

These papers are generally treated as disposable media. Reuse would require a demonstrated cleaning, integrity and contamination-control procedure.
