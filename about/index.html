About Sri Bharathi Filtration Division

An Indian manufacturer and supplier of industrial filter pads, papers, cartridges and lenticular systems, focused on process fit, controlled media properties and custom requirements.

Process feedClarified filtrate

Company

Filtration experience grounded in industrial practice

Sri Bharathi Tech is part of the Sri Bharathi Group of Companies. Its filtration division manufactures and supplies sparkler pulp filter pads, filter papers, lenticular systems and multiple cartridge constructions for chemical, pharmaceutical, beverage, distillery, food and other process industries.

Existing company information describes a founder with more than 40 years of industrial experience, supported by research, development and custom specification work. The current portfolio includes wood-pulp, cotton-pulp and hybrid-pulp pads; PP pleated, spun-bonded and wound cartridges; stainless-steel elements; and pleated dust collectors.

How we approach a new duty

We begin with process conditions, filter geometry and the required result. This narrows a trial candidate and the documents needed for technical approval.

Operating values

Useful filtration starts with honest specification

01

Application fit

Recommendations are based on fluid, temperature, solids, equipment and quality target.

02

Customisation

Dimensions and selected media parameters can be reviewed against user drawings and samples.

03

Process control

Published controls include porosity, filtration speed, absorbency, efficiency and bursting factor.

04

Evidence over slogans

Unverified ratings, approvals and alpha-cellulose figures are not published as facts.

05

Technical continuity

Related pads, papers, modules and cartridges can be considered as one filtration train.

Two locations
