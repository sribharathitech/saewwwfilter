Document status

Current evidence comes before certification claims

The legacy Sri Bharathi website references an “ISO 9001-2000 Certified manufacturing unit” and a Dun & Bradstreet certified manufacturing unit. ISO 9001:2000 is a superseded edition, and no current certificate file, scope, certificate number, issuer or validity date was publicly available during this website rebuild.

For accuracy, this new site does not present those legacy statements as current certifications. Customers should request controlled copies of current documents and verify the legal entity, manufacturing location, scope, issuing body and expiry date before relying on them.

No implied product approval

A management-system certificate does not automatically certify every filter grade for food, pharmaceutical, sterile or other regulated use.

Due diligence

What to verify on a certificate

01

Legal entity

The named organisation should match the contracting or manufacturing entity.

02

Site and scope

Check that the relevant plant and filtration activities are within scope.

03

Standard edition

Confirm the current standard and any applicable transition status.

04

Issuer

Verify accreditation and certificate authenticity with the issuing body.

05

Validity

Review issue, expiry and surveillance status.

06

Product documents

Obtain separate grade-specific contact, material or validation evidence.
