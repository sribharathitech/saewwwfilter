Control points

From raw material to converted filtration media

Sri Bharathi's published manufacturing information describes virgin pulp inputs, automated filter-pad production and reduced direct handling from pulping to finished media. Raw materials are monitored and routine tests are stated for filtration efficiency, pad porosity, absorbency, filtration speed and bursting factor.

Applicable paper and pad fibres receive a porous, chemically inert wet-strength treatment intended to preserve mechanical strength during aqueous filtration. The relevant grade specification should identify which controls apply and how acceptance is recorded.

Qualification

What a useful quality package should contain

01

Approved specification

Media, dimensions, tolerances and declared performance tied to a product code.

02

Test methods

Units and procedures for porosity, strength, flow or retention claims.

03

Lot traceability

Manufacturing identification linked to release documentation.

04

Material declarations

Grade-specific fibre, polymer, alloy and contact information as applicable.

05

Change control

Agreed communication for material or process changes affecting the approved grade.

06

Application trial

Documented performance in the user's representative process before routine supply.

Customer responsibility

Confirm suitability in the real process

Published catalogue values narrow product choice; they do not replace user qualification. Customers should verify compatibility, retention, extractables, microbial requirements, pressure, temperature, cleaning and applicable regulatory obligations.

For sensitive applications, request the latest technical data sheet and supporting documents with the quotation. Define any certificate fields or acceptance limits before the first production order.
