Activated Carbon Filter Pads

Activated carbon-impregnated filter media for processes that may benefit from contained adsorption and particulate retention in a single supported pad.

Operating principle

How the filtration product works

An activated carbon filter pad incorporates adsorptive carbon within a supported filter matrix. As a compatible liquid passes through the pad, suspended particles can be retained by the media while selected dissolved colour- or odour-causing compounds may adsorb onto available carbon surface.

This replaces two separate operations in some plate-and-frame or supported-pad processes: dosing loose powdered carbon into a tank and subsequently removing that powder on paper or cloth. Adsorption capacity is finite, so colour or odour breakthrough—not only differential pressure—can determine the endpoint.

Selection starts with the process.

State the liquid or gas, operating temperature, target retention, batch volume, viscosity, solids load, housing geometry and cleaning or disposal requirement.

Media design

Construction and process behaviour

Sri Bharathi publishes carbon-impregnated pads made with different activated-carbon grades to help optimise the filtration duty. The detailed carbon type, loading, binder or support chemistry, dimensions and extractables profile are grade-specific and should be requested before trial.

Because adsorption is molecule-specific, an activated carbon pad cannot be selected solely by particle-retention language. Contact time, concentration, temperature, pH, competing organics, flow and carbon accessibility all influence treatment performance.

Process fit

Applications and selection considerations

Activated carbon pads may be considered where colour, odour or trace organic reduction is needed alongside clarification in compatible chemical, beverage, food and process-liquid duties. Suitability depends on whether the target compound adsorbs effectively and whether the pad materials meet product-contact requirements.

Qualification approach

  • Define the target compound or measurable colour/odour criterion.
  • Record feed concentration, temperature, pH and flow per unit area.
  • Sample across the run to establish a breakthrough curve.
  • Measure any initial rinse requirement and product yield loss.
  • Check for carbon fines, extractables and interaction with valuable product components.

Questions engineers ask

Frequently asked questions

Is an activated carbon pad equivalent to adding powdered activated carbon?

Not exactly. Carbon dose, accessible surface area, contact pattern and residence time differ. A side-by-side process trial is needed.

Can activated carbon remove every colour or odour?

No. Adsorption is selective and affected by molecular size, polarity, pH, temperature and competing substances.

When should an activated carbon pad be changed?

Use an agreed outlet-quality or breakthrough limit, together with pressure and throughput criteria. Pressure alone may not reveal exhausted adsorption capacity.

Does the activated carbon pad eliminate downstream filtration?

It can combine functions in some systems, but a polishing filter may still be required depending on fines control and final cleanliness.
